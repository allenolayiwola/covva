# Addendum 6 — Additional features (Phase 3–4 unless noted)

1. Micro and on-demand insurance
- Daily, weekly and monthly low-cost policies for okada/trotro/ride-hailing drivers, market traders, artisans and event organisers.
- Fast purchase and renewal via mobile money, WhatsApp and USSD.
- Product model must support short policy periods and pay-as-you-go premiums.

2. Local language support
- Full UI localisation for English, Twi, Ga and Ewe (extensible to others).
- Voice-note support for key flows (quote help, claims description) with transcription.

3. Plain-language policy explainer (AI assistant)
- Assistant that answers "am I covered if...?" questions grounded strictly in the actual policy wording and schedule the customer holds.
- Must cite the relevant clause, avoid giving regulated advice, and hand off to the licensed broker for anything advisory.

4. Motor telematics discounts
- Optional phone-based driving behaviour scoring (speeding, harsh braking, night driving) with explicit consent.
- Safe-driver discounts applied at renewal where the insurer supports it.

5. Post-claim partner network
- Directory of approved garages, towing services and hospitals/clinics.
- Direct billing to insurer where agreed, so customers do not pay upfront.
- Partner onboarding, ratings and job tracking linked to claims.

6. Loyalty and rewards
- No-claims rewards, multi-policy bundle discounts, referral credits and renewal loyalty perks.
- Rules engine so rewards can be configured per insurer.

7. Funeral cover and welfare groups
- Funeral cover products integrated with family, church, association and welfare groups (in Ghana and diaspora).
- Group admin features: member enrolment, contribution collection, beneficiary records and claims support.
- Link with the diaspora sponsor model so relatives abroad can fund cover for family in Ghana.
