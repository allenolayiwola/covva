# Addendum 1 — Missing features (accepted in full)

Before pilot launch:
* Insurer product data for real partners (the current catalogue is fictional demo products).
* Real NIC licence verification, Ghana Card identity checks through a provider (now a manual admin review), and a sanctions/AML screening step for business customers.
* Policy document generation: branded PDF schedules and receipts for customers. Insurers currently upload their own files.
* Premium financing/instalments via mobile money, which the strategy flags for informal-sector customers.
* A WhatsApp channel for notifications and basic servicing, called out in the proposal.
* Localisation: English only today; the strategy mentions Twi/Ga/Ewe for accessibility.

Operational depth:
* Insurer connectors (Level 2 quote API and Level 3 policy API) beyond the Level 1 portal. The framework exists but no live insurer adapter does.
* Broker performance scorecards and routing weighted by service quality, with customer-facing broker ratings once there's enough data.
* Accounting and reconciliation exports (statements per insurer, commission reports for brokers, VAT and levy handling on premiums).
* Endorsement workflows mid-policy (change vehicle, add a driver) as structured flows rather than free-text requests.
* Group and employer schemes: member enrolment and bulk policy administration (the enterprise plan exists but has no screens).
